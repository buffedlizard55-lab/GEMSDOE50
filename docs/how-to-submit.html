Manual steps for a future eligible artifact

  1. Use only a build explicitly marked SLOT-ELIGIBLE after spatial holdout, matched controls, provenance/license review, and byte-level format validation. None is eligible now.
  2. Download the single-band float32 GeoTIFF and verify the listed SHA-256.
  3. Re-check the official format: EPSG:32611, 100 m, same bounds, [0,1] inside valid bounds, null/NaN outside.
  4. Confirm external-data rights for both challenge use and sharing with the sponsor; prepare required attribution and generative-AI disclosure.
  5. Sign in to DrivenData manually, choose an available weekly slot only if the owner approves, and upload the validated file without altering it.
  6. Save the portal receipt and associate its score with the exact file hash. Do not claim acceptance or a score without that receipt.
