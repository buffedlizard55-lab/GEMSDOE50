Why h33-h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778 — and whether more is achievable

Verification labels: [OFFICIAL] from an organizer source · [MEASURED] computed here from hash-pinned bytes · [MODEL] arithmetic under stated assumptions · [PROJECT] the owner's own records.

1. The metric, exactly [OFFICIAL]

k(d)  = max(1 − d/300 m, 0)                            3 px at 100 m
TP_w  = Σ_g max_{x: d(x,g)≤R} p(x)·k(d(x,g))
FP_w  = Σ_{x:p(x)>0} p(x)·[1 − max_g k(d(x,g))]
FN_w  = Σ_g [1 − max_{x: d(x,g)≤R} p(x)·k(d(x,g))]
DTI   = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·FN_w + ε)

The identity TP_w + FN_w = |G| follows because the same maximum appears in both sums, so DTI = T / (0.8G + 0.2(T+F)). The repository's test suite reproduces the page's own worked example to 1e-12 and checks the identity numerically.

  • Binary beats graded. A support scaled by λ∈(0,1) is strictly dominated: dDTI/dλ = 0.8·T·G/(0.2λ(T+F)+0.8G)² > 0. Every competitive file is binary.
  • Marginal-inclusion rule. One dot raises the denominator by exactly 0.2, so it pays iff k > 0.2·score — within 283 m of hidden truth at 0.2778, 277 m at the current 0.3774 best. It is a distance filter, not a precision filter.
  • Coverage arithmetic. x = s(0.2ρ+0.8)/(1−0.2s), ρ = F/G. A score of 0.3774 needs 32.7 % weighted coverage with no false positives, 40.8 % at ρ = 1.

5. Is a higher score achievable? Yes — and the gap is coverage, not emission quality

With G ≈ 13,833, perfect knowledge at 300 m spacing gives T ≈ 0.8G and DTI ≈ 0.78. The step from 0.28 to 0.38 is therefore a coverage problem: find more of the faults the catalogue lacks, and put the dots exactly there. The lever is evidence independent of the catalogue, because the scored truth is by construction a different inventory. That is precisely what this project's leading component (1 m-LiDAR scarp chains) does, and it is why it is the leading term of the shipped field.

6. The hypotheses, ranked

  1. H50-A LiDAR scarp-dipole chains — 12-channel 1 m-DEM scarp stack; signature √(step·facing)·√(coherence) with a 16-direction chain vote. Catches sub-100 m strands that regional mapping cannot see. Validated: 0.0345 vs 0.0190 random off-catalogue at equal budget.
  2. H50-B seismicity-lineament corridors — official USGS ComCat epicentres; 2-D inertia-tensor orientation with a Poisson nearest-neighbour crossover. [FALSIFIED] at equal budget (0.0015–0.0034 vs 0.0016–0.0043 for smoothed density). Retained at low weight as a hedge only.
  3. H50-C multi-physics oriented-lineament consensus — Hessian line response × orientation order parameter across eight official physics bands. Best on the catalogue frame (0.0217), weak off-catalogue (0.0087).
  4. H50-D depth-resolved lineations — proposed: the 3-D inertia-tensor plane test removes the swarm confounder that falsified H50-B. Data in hand; pre-specified test.
  5. H50-E fluid-path alignment — proposed: chains of GDR thermal features aligned on a conductive-basement gradient ridge. Data in hand; pre-specified test.

Full table with layers, novelty and falsification criteria: docs/research/hypotheses.md.

7. Regularities flagged for review

  • The live leaderboard changed while this project ran: the brief cites 0.3195 as the best public score; on 2026-10-06 the top score is 0.3774 and 0.3195 is seventh. Every target statement should be read against the fetched date.
  • Public scores cluster at ≈0.32 for six entrants and drop sharply at rank 8 (0.2888) — a bimodal board, consistent with two families of method (dotted supports vs. probability maps).
  • The 0.2600 file (44,090 dots) and the 0.2778 file (37,654 dots) differ only by pruning near the catalogue, which implies the scored inventory is nearly disjoint from the published catalogue — a strong, checkable clue about the test set.
