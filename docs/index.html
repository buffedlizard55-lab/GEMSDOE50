Expectation, not a promise. The holdout scores a proxy (USGS SGMC faults away from the given catalogue), not the organizers’ hidden expert labels. The calibrated expectation for H57 is about 0.23 (P(> 0.2778) ≈ 29 %, P(> 0.3774) ≈ 4 %). Under the corrected metric algebra, beating the public best 0.3774 with 80,000 dots needs about 10,500 hidden-truth credit (credit/dot > 0.13). No organizer score exists for any GEMSDOE50 file.

Uniqueness, measured against the full prior corpus (302 distinct submission-like files from all 54 sibling repositories plus this one). H57 is not a copy: its largest exact-pixel overlap (Jaccard) with any prior file is 0.1716, and that file is our own never-submitted H56; at most 31 % of its dots sit on any single prior file. But 27,248 of its 80,000 dots (34 %) land on a pixel that at least one of the 61 registered prior files also used, and its chance-corrected 2-pixel proximity to the dense 13GEMSDOE r8-ensemble is 0.5084 (borderline against a 0.5 screen), because every scarp-based file follows the same LiDAR scarps. A version with zero exact overlap was tested and scores lower (0.2088), so it is not recommended.

Today’s run (H59): the required seismic-lineation map — NO SLOT

Built as specified: 63,081 screened USGS ComCat earthquakes, declustered with the Zaliapin–Ben-Zion nearest-neighbour method (12,450 background events kept), a 2-D triangle test (my unverified adaptation of the Ouillon–Sornette 3-D tetrahedron test), local 2-D covariance with location-error removal, corridors as wide as the catalogue location error plus up-dip copies, and dots snapped to the H57 ridge. Result: only 31 lineations and 326 dots; holdout DTI 0.0002, credit/dot 0.0232 versus 0.1267 for random dots. On withheld catalogue faults the corridors lose to smoothed density (0.0007 vs 0.0093). Within every H57-strength decile, the corridors hold less fault credit than the cells outside them (median ratio 0.065, 0/10 deciles above 1). Loosening every screen (up to 1,034 lineations) never lifts that ratio above 0.97. Two variants were also rejected: mixing the corridor dots into H57 (0.2088) and spacing H57’s dots 4 or 5 px apart (0.1866, 0.1786). The H59 file is unique (Jaccard ≤ 0.0016) and valid, but do not submit it: H59 research file (SHA-256 009dd3d8dffae29d…, entry name if ever used GEMSDOE50-H59-UPDIPSEIS-326-CC1F44DA).
