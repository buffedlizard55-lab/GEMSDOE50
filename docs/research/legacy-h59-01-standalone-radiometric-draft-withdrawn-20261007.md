## Historical H59-01 test sketch (unapproved and withdrawn; do not execute)

### Data and feature recipe

1. Input: the four-band GeoDAWN radiometric mirror, band 4 `TC`; SHA-256 must equal `c22420f75999030d7cc65c9e31e50d232ea6158423bca051613a18a8b20ba682`. Record bytes, band description/tags, source URL, checksum, and the fact that this is a sibling transport mirror rather than a fresh official download. If hash/grid/description differs, stop.
2. Valid cells: finite sample-template footprint AND positive TC source mask. Use no survey labels to set feature thresholds.
3. For each fixed scale σ ∈ {1.5, 3.0} pixels, calculate the TC gradient magnitude after Gaussian smoothing and multiply by a σ=3.0 structure-tensor coherence field. Take the pixelwise maximum across the two scales and normalize by the 98th percentile over eligible source-valid footprint cells. Do not tune scale, percentile, coherence, or score after observing holdout results.
4. Emission: exactly 80,000 binary unit dots from the frozen feature score, seed `590007`, with a verified Euclidean minimum separation of 3.0 pixels (300 m). Apply only the published-grid mask, the 300 m public-catalogue buffer, and the exact prior-pixel exclusion. If 80,000 dots cannot be emitted under these constraints, report the achieved mass and treat the fixed-mass gate as failed; do not lower spacing or widen the domain.
5. Build both required raster semantics: (a) all-finite zero-outside-footprint twin for the reported range-parser failure mode, with **every** cell re-read finite and in `[0,1]`; (b) NaN-outside-footprint twin to match the published sample semantics. Do not claim both are accepted by the portal; no upload occurs until the portal's no-data/range behavior is resolved.

### Frozen holdout, controls, and promotion gate

- Use `evidence/holdout-v1.json` unchanged (split SHA-256 recorded in the result): four NW/NE/SW/SE half-open macrofolds, 10 km eroded cores, and 300 m buffer from visible training-label pixels. The independent proxy truth is USGS SGMC fault pixels more than 300 m from the supplied catalogue, restricted to each held-out core. Reconstruct eligibility from the official label raster and template only; never use held-out truth to construct the feature or emitter.
- Primary comparator: the frozen 80,000-dot H57 incumbent. Report its actual active dot count in each eligible core. Evaluate the candidate, incumbent, and controls with the exact published distance-weighted Tversky components and the same truth/domain masks; aggregate TP/FP/FN components rather than averaging fold scores.
- Controls at candidate-matched active mass within each fold: (i) the smoothed TC magnitude field (Gaussian σ=3 px; distinguishes edges from source brightness), (ii) five fixed-seed uniform blue-noise/Poisson-disk draws, and (iii) four fixed 5 km translations of the candidate score field, re-emitted at the same fold mass. Report any active-count shortfall rather than silently comparing unequal masses.
- Bootstrap paired deltas on the frozen 16 spatial subtiles (5,000 replicates, seed `590008`). No pixelwise bootstrap.
- **Promotion gate—all clauses required:** candidate pooled DTI must exceed H57 by at least +0.002; paired 95% block-bootstrap interval for candidate minus H57 must be strictly positive; candidate must win in at least 3/4 macrofolds; it must exceed the smoothed-TC, matched-random, and translated controls at equal active mass; format and exact/proximity novelty gates must pass; source rights/provenance and sponsor-sharing must be cleared. Any failure means `NO SLOT`; no parameter revision against this holdout is permitted.
