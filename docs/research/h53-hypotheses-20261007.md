# H53 hypothesis ranking — frozen before implementation

**Registered:** 2026-10-07 UTC. This review ranks four genuinely different, testable physical signals before any H53 detector was implemented or scored. It is a prior, not a score forecast. The official GEMS task is to predict faults absent from the current public catalogue; the official external-data condition is that participants must hold a licence allowing the data to be used in the challenge and shared with the sponsor for evaluation. No candidate is eligible for a scoring slot unless the source, grid, controls, and blocked-holdout gates all pass.

## Explicitly not ranked as a new candidate: seismicity-lineation adaptation

The point-geometry version of the seismicity idea is **not** being presented as new or validated. H51-B already implements event-geometry corridors and was level with or below matched controls on the accessible off-catalogue proxy; the seismicity-only family was not given mass in the current candidate. The prior work is not proof that every event-pattern method fails, but a re-run is not justified without a materially different, pre-registered experiment. Any future test must use point-pattern geometry rather than density alone; account for declustering, induced events near injection/mining sites, event-location uncertainty, exclusion of the known-fault buffer, and matched 1 km/2 km smoothed-density controls. The mixed-network ComCat export's source-specific rights remain unresolved. This branch does not re-use that extract.

## Decision

H53-A is the only candidate advanced to a **single fixed implementation** in this review because its official source and license are clear at the landing-page level, its relevant files are already present in ignored scratch storage, and its geometry differs from existing code. It remains **provisional** until the mirror bytes are matched to the official releases. No parameter sweep or holdout tuning is authorized. H53-B through H53-D remain research proposals; none is implemented or measured here.
