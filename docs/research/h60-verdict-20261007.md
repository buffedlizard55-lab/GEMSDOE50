# H60 verdict — official-stack belief field, the union arm, and the price of novelty (2026-10-07)

**One-line result.** H60 asked whether the organizer 19-band stack carries off-catalogue fault
information that the LiDAR/topographic-step families behind H57 do not, and whether a *maximally*
novel placement can be built that still scores. The first answer is "it carries information, but no
usable increment": the union arm is a statistical tie with the incumbent. The second answer is "no,
and the price is measurable" — the maximal-novelty control loses to a matched uniform control on the
frozen gate. H60 is therefore recorded **NO SLOT** and **H57 stays the recommended submission**.

## 1. What H60 screened (measured)

`scripts/h60_screen.py` (`evidence/h60_screen.json`) ran the sparse-dot emitter at 30,000 dots over
17 `training_features.tif` channels in three transforms each (`raw`, `grad`, `ridge2`) and scored
them on the off-catalogue F1 frame (USGS SGMC fault pixels more than 300 m from the given
catalogue), inside **five elevation strata** — the stratifier exists because F1 truth sits in the
mountains, so an unstratified screen mostly selects terrain.

Winners (pooled lift over uniform, strata won):

* `raw::det_elev_slope` **2.198** (5/5) and `raw::det_elev` **2.193** (5/5)
* `grad::det_elev_slope` **2.064** (5/5), `grad::tc` **1.969** (5/5)
* `raw::iso_grav_anom` **1.909** (5/5), `ridge2::geod_dilaterate` **1.887** (5/5)
* `ridge2::det_elev_slope` **1.877** (5/5), `ridge2::tc` **1.491** (5/5)

Failures: every magnetic amplitude channel (0.50–0.98), the shipped gravity-gradient products
(0.31–1.29), raw geodetic strain (0.28–0.38), `depth_to_base_surf` (0.33–1.18), `cond_surf`
(1.415, 3/5 strata). This **corrects an older claim** in this repository that official raw bands
universally lose to the owner-derived LiDAR stack: detrended elevation and its slope do carry
off-catalogue information, and the earlier negative finding was an artifact of the unstratified
screen.

## 2. The belief field and the mass sweep (measured)

`scripts/h60_sweep.py` (`evidence/h60_sweep.json`) built the H60 belief as the equal-weight mean of
four family rank-means — `official_topography` (raw `det_elev`, raw/grad/ridge2 `det_elev_slope`),
`official_magnetics` (grad/ridge2 `tc`), `official_gravity` (raw `iso_grav_anom`), `official_geodesy`
(ridge2 `geod_dilaterate`) — and swept mass on the F1 frame:

| dots | F1 credit/dot | F1 lift | F1 DTI | model mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 37,654 | 0.0980 | 2.33 | 0.0649 | 0.203 |
| 50,000 | 0.0919 | 2.19 | 0.0775 | **0.206 (argmax)** |
| 80,000 | 0.0822 | 1.95 | 0.1006 | 0.201 |

Model means are the arithmetic mean of the two fitted hidden-frame models and are **not scores**.
The official-stack belief peaks at lower mass than the morphology field does, which is the first sign
that the two fields disagree about where the marginal dot is worth placing.

## 3. Arms on the frozen gate (measured)

`scripts/h60_arms.py` (`evidence/h60_arms.json`) compared arms at matched mass on four macrofolds;
`scripts/validate_h56.py --truth-mode sgmc_off` re-measured the built artifacts on the repository's
frozen spatially blocked gate. Pooled DTI (matched uniform control in brackets):

| candidate | dots | frozen gate | uniform | folds positive | paired 95 % CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| main's H59 candidate (sibling session) | 90,000 | **0.2125** | 0.1286 | 4/4 | [+0.0557, +0.1105] |
| H60-union-d0 | 75,308 | **0.2033** | 0.1050 | 4/4 | [+0.0662, +0.1317] |
| H57-scarpstep (previous recommendation) | 80,000 | 0.2025 | 0.1053 | 4/4 | [+0.0626, +0.1330] |
| H60-union-d2 (max novelty) | 75,308 | 0.0618 | 0.0917 | **0/4** | [−0.0436, −0.0159] |
| H60-official-stack | 50,000 | 0.1224 | 0.0749 | 4/4 | [+0.0063, +0.0850] |

On the second instrument (`scripts/h60_validate.py`, pooled whole-map estimator on the same F1
truth, with matched-mass uniform and translation controls) the union arm scores **0.1018** against
H57's 0.0914 and the sibling session's H59 candidate's **0.1255** (uniform ceiling 0.0421).

**Measured ranking on the shared frozen gate: main's H59 0.2125 > H60-union 0.2033 > H57 0.2025.**
So H60-union beats the older H57 on both instruments but does **not** beat the candidate already
merged on main; the decision is **NO SLOT** for that reason, not because the artifact fails.

**Prior-pixel audit (new, and it changes what may be submitted).** Counting dots that land on cells
of the frozen 61-file / 1,405,451-cell prior-positive union: **H57 27,248/80,000 (34.1 %)**,
**H56 27,914/90,000 (31.0 %)**, H60-union 0, H58 0, the maximal-novelty control 0, main's H59 0. The
standing charter forbids reusing prior prediction pixels, so H57 and H56 are history: they remain
useful comparators but must not be presented as submission candidates, and the H57 band's "no pixel
of any prior submission is copied" sentence (computed against a 26-file subset) is superseded.

## 4. Hidden-frame models (modelled; three of them disagree)

Applying the three models fitted on the 21 corpus artifacts with recorded scores
(`evidence/h57_hidden_model.json`) to both candidates:

| model | H57 @80,000 | H60-union @75,308 | direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| transfer `h = 0.652·F1_c/dot` (r² = −0.87, retracted) | 0.1428 | 0.1623 | union |
| mass `h = e^{4.762}·N^{−0.671}` (r² = 0.515) | 0.1761 | 0.1788 | union (marginal) |
| both `h = 0.5305 − 0.1669·F1 − 0.0390·ln N` (r² = 0.651) | **0.2286** | 0.2237 | H57 |

The models **disagree in sign**, so no direction is established. Exceedance under the `both` model:
H57 P(>0.2778) = 0.286, P(>0.3774) = 0.043; H60-union P(>0.2778) = 0.261, P(>0.3774) = 0.035. Two
further caveats, both material:

1. The `both` model's F1 coefficient is **negative and not significant** (t = −1.05): it encodes the
   corpus confound that the artifacts with the highest proxy credit were *not* the highest scorers,
   not a mechanism by which better placement lowers the hidden score.
2. Applied to the maximal-novelty control the same model predicts 0.2518 — the **highest** of all
   three candidates — while that control actually scores 0.0618 on the frozen gate and loses to
   uniform in 4/4 folds. A model that ranks the worst artifact best is an ordering heuristic inside
   the corpus's feature range, not a transfer function. It is reported here for completeness and is
   not used to choose between the two files.

## 5. The finding that matters: novelty past the corpus is bought with score

The charter forbids copying prior prediction pixels, so H60 measured what the *strict* form of that
rule costs. Two arms were built from the same belief field and the same mass:

* **d0** — no dot may coincide with a prior positive cell (the ordinary construction). Result: 0 of
  1,405,451 prior cells reused; 0.437 of dots are more than 2 px from every prior dot; frozen gate
  **0.2033**.
* **d2** — a Euclidean disk of radius 2 around every prior positive cell is excluded, so *every* dot
  is more than 2 px from every prior dot (novel fraction **0.714**, gate ≥ 0.5 satisfied). Result:
  frozen gate **0.0618**, uniform control 0.0917, **0/4** macrofolds positive; the F1 lift collapses
  from 2.07× to **0.67×** — worse than uniform.

The prior corpus and the owner-derived proxies agree on where the plausible terrain is. A rule that
requires dots to keep more than 2 px of clearance from every prior submission pixel — which is what
this repository's own 0.5 novelty threshold implies — is therefore **score-destructive**, and the
threshold should be re-read as a copy detector, not a placement constraint. Neither H57 (0.315) nor
H60-union (0.437) meets 0.5; both meet the binding constraint that actually protects other entries:
**zero reused prior pixels**, and a maximum full-pixel IoU of 0.204 (against this project's own H57)
with no byte-identical file in the corpus.

* `docs/downloads/checks-gemsdoe50-h60-union-d0-75308-20261007T2250Z.json`: PASS at block-signature
  level; no identical SHA-256; worst block-32 IoU 0.678 against `gems32-bayesopt-dilation-scarp-d28`
  (block-occupancy signature only — it is a coarse screen, and the fine-grained gate is
  `evidence/h60_uniqueness-d0.json`).
* `evidence/h60_uniqueness-d0.json`: 30 files compared on disk, max full-pixel IoU **0.204**
  (worst overlap = our own H57), min novel fraction **0.437**, verdict at the 0.5 threshold: false
  (see §5).
* Format: single band, float32, EPSG:32611, 3,730 × 3,292, 100 m, origin (243350, 4508550); all
  12,279,160 cells finite and in [0, 1] in the primary file (the direct antidote to the
  `Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]` portal failure); 0 dots within 300 m of the given
  catalogue; minimum Chebyshev spacing 3 px, so the dots are at the metric's own support.

## 7. Limitations and irregularities (for review)

1. **No committed preregistration for H60.** H55 and H58 have preregistration documents; H60 does
   not. Its design record is the receipt chain (`h60_screen.json` → `h60_sweep.json` →
   `h60_arms.json` → builds → holdout), which shows what was run but not what was frozen in advance.
   The arms and the novelty control were defined **after** the screen was seen, so they are
   exploratory. Treat the H60 numbers as hypothesis-generating, not confirmatory.
2. **The tie is a tie.** +0.0008 on the frozen gate is inside the instrument's noise; the median
   count that would make it decisive is not available without a slot. Nothing here justifies
   spending one.
3. **F1 truth is a surrogate.** It is USGS SGMC fault pixels more than 300 m from the given
   catalogue — mapped inventory, not the hidden geothermal label set. A candidate can win this frame
   and lose the competition; the corpus calibration in `evidence/h57_hidden_model.json` is the only
   bridge, and §4 shows how weak it is.
4. **No organizer score, no upload, no leaderboard polling.** No weekly slot was used; nothing on the
   site should be read as a score.
5. **Rights.** The morphology family rests on owner-mirrored publicly documented layers (USGS 3DEP
   LiDAR derivatives, open topographic composites) with the chain disclosed in the build receipt; the
   H60 official-stack family rests on the organizer's own `training_features.tif`. Neither needs
   ComCat, so the unresolved mixed-network question that blocks H56 does not apply here.
6. The earlier "official raw bands lose" claim is superseded by §1, and the "0.386 hidden estimate"
   that once appeared on the site came from the retracted transfer factor — both are corrected in the
   regenerated pages.

## 8. Decision

* **H60-union: NO SLOT.** It beats the older H57 on both instruments but ranks second to the
  candidate merged on main (0.2033 vs 0.2125 on the same frozen gate), and the hidden-frame models
  disagree about the direction. It is published as a documented, unique, portal-safe alternative —
  it is not forbidden to submit, it is simply not the file this repository ranks first.
* **The recommended submission is the repository's current candidate, `gemsdoe50-h59-sharpened-scarp-scatter-90k-20261007T171954Z-allfinite.tif`** (0.2125 on the shared frozen gate, 0 prior cells reused).
* H57-scarpstep-80000 is **history only** (34.1 % of its dots sit on prior-submission cells). (`docs/downloads/gemsdoe50-h57-scarpstep-80000-20261007T1830Z-allfinite.tif`, SHA-256
  `8027c4e9fe0f789e2d7180a7ccc031abec90b391cf9e42ebcc125752af9fb696`).
* **Next-session priority** is now the *mass axis on the frozen gate*, not more channel families:
  the sweep (§2) and the arms (§3) disagree about the optimum mass, the frozen gate is the only
  instrument with a matched control at every mass, and it is cheap (~45 s per point). Until that
  curve is measured, H57's mass is the incumbent default rather than a verified optimum.
