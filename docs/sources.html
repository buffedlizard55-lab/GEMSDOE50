Official prize rules (PDF) Up to three submissions per week; one final submission per entity; prize structure; AI use permitted with disclosure.
Staff clarification — masked known pixels The known-fault mask is pixel-exact and identical to the provided labels with no buffer; a new-truth pixel can lie within 300 m of a known trace (corrections/modifications count). Basis of the 100–400 m correction corridor.
Staff clarification — pooled scoring Public and private scores pool all subset pixels into one Tversky index; the final round uses the whole GeoDAWN area; “new fault” = any fault pixel not already captured by USGS/INGENIOUS.
Hermant et al. 2025 (cited by the organisers) In north-central Nevada, USGS Quaternary fault traces differ from expert/lidar labels by up to 400 m. The correction corridor's distance band.
GDR / OpenEI submission 1391 (INGENIOUS) 1,126 in-footprint fault traces with map scale: 18.1 % at 1:100,000 (≈ 50 m nominal accuracy), 81.9 % at 1:250,000 (≈ 125 m).
USGS FDSN/ComCat The earthquake catalogue used for the lineation detector (222,939 events in the study window; USGS data are public domain, no credential needed).
GeoDAWN airborne radiometrics (USGS 22103) K, Th, U, total-count channels on the competition grid; used for the radiometric lineament evidence.
USGS 3DEP Source of the 1 m lidar DEM from which the scarp descriptors (step/laplacian/coherence) were computed; USGS products carry no use restrictions.
Ouillon, Ducorbier & Sornette 2008 · Ouillon & Sornette 2011 · Wang et al. 2013 The anisotropy clustering method (minimise Σλ₃², split until λ₃ < Δ) and the tetrahedra-randomisation clustered/background separation that the declustering step adapts to 2-D. The adaptation is ours and is labelled as such in the code.
Woessner & Wiemer 2005 · Zaliapin & Ben-Zion 2013 · Baiesi & Paczuski 2004 Magnitude-of-completeness estimation (maximum curvature + 0.2) and the rescaled nearest-neighbour contrast used as the independent declustering control; all with published default parameters, never tuned on any label.
Organisers' reference solution A U-Net with Tversky loss (α = 0.2, β = 0.8) — used only to confirm the metric parameterisation.
