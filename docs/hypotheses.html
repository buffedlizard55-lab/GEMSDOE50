Each hypothesis names (a) the layer(s), (b) the physical signature and transform, (c) why it can catch a fault missing from the given catalogue rather than one already in it, and (d) how it differs from everything already implemented in the sibling GEMSDOE repositories. Ranking is expected DTI improvement per unit of implementation cost; measured numbers are credit per emitted cell at matched mass, which is the only mass-free comparison the published metric allows.

H50-1 — legacy 2-D seismicity lineation corridors ALREADY IMPLEMENTED; NOT NEW; COMCAT RIGHTS UNRESOLVED

  • (a) Layers. Historical mixed-network ComCat export (222,939 rows, FDSN event service; contributor-specific rights and competition shareability unresolved) → MS≥1.5, depth ≤ 20 km, 1970+, 18,142 events inside the survey; plus the USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM scarp descriptors for placement only.
  • (b) Transform (legacy code audit). The builder computes the project's unverified 2-D triangle-area adaptation of the Ouillon & Sornette tetrahedron test, but does not apply its returned keep mask to subsequent lineation fits; the separate nearest-neighbour declustering function is not called by the builder. It then clusters in (x, y, 3 km/yr · t) with DBSCAN, split each cluster by recursive 2-means until its minor-axis σ ≤ Δ = 600 m (the Ouillon–Ducorbier–Sornette recursion), keep corridors with λ₁/λ₂ ≥ 3 by Monte-Carlo p < 0.01, emit the axis, the curved spine, and 3 px gap bridges; snap every corridor cell to the local maximum of the lidar scarp ridge within 300 m.
  • (c) Why it finds what the catalogue lacks. The given raster is a mapped-trace inventory. Epicentral lineaments sit 1–4 km off those traces (measured: median 32.7 px to the nearest catalogue cell; only 0.8× enrichment of events inside 300 m of the catalogue versus the base rate, i.e. no enrichment). A fault that has not been mapped has still been rupturing, and its microseismicity is in the catalogue regardless.
  • (d) Novelty correction. The old claim that no prior submission contained seismicity-derived mass is withdrawn: the 2-D covariance/lineation family is already implemented in this repository and is not new. The sibling repositories list “seismic-density lineaments against a Poisson null” as an unimplemented hypothesis (GEMSDOE40 §4, “H45”), and their seismic bands (ieq_n100a15/deq_n100a15) are self-correlated at 3 km, i.e. near-constant across the 300 m scoring kernel; the raw catalogue is the resolution upgrade, and the corridor is a lineation, not a density.
  • Measured. Credit/cell 0.2227 on the off-catalogue SGMC instrument (incumbent 0.1434 = 1.55×) and 0.0022 on the 2020 Monte Cristo rupture trend (incumbent 0.0008, and 0.0000 for smoothed density and uniform random at matched mass).

H50-2 — gap closure and splay continuation PARTLY IN SHIPPED FILE

  • (a) The same corridor set, plus catalogue trace endpoints.
  • (b) Where two corridor axes are within 1.5 km and ≤ 40° apart, emit a sparse bridge (one dot per 300 m); likewise continue a catalogue trace along its local strike to the nearest seismicity corridor endpoint.
  • (c) The hidden set is “faults not in the current public database”; a large fraction of such faults are extensions or splays of mapped faults rather than isolated structures.
  • (d) GEMSDOE40’s H44 asks for the same closure but bridges with Euler depth clusters (gravity/magnetic). Bridging with epicentral spines is a different, cheaper evidence stream; it is in the shipped file as 3 connectors plus 37 spines.
  • Measured. Bridges added 3 segments; their isolated effect is unmeasured because the greedy pack absorbs them — flagged as a limitation.

H50-3 — cross-inventory disagreement MEASURED, NOT SHIPPED

  • (a) USGS SGMC state compilation and NBMG QFaults/INGENIOUS traces absent from the given catalogue raster (61,664 cells beyond 300 m).
  • (b) No transform: emit the traces themselves.
  • (c) These are faults mapped by state/USGS compilations that the competition’s raster omits — which is exactly the definition of the hidden set, unless the organisers used the same compilations (in which case they are already “known” and score as false positives).
  • (d) The sibling family used SGMC only as a validation instrument, never as an emission layer.
  • Measured. 1.29 credit/cell at 30,000 cells on the SGMC-off instrument (the instrument’s arithmetic ceiling is 1.63 at that mass, so the layer is nearly perfect by construction — a self-fulfilling instrument, which is precisely why it is not shipped: it cannot be distinguished from leakage into the organiser’s own label source.)

H50-4 — radiometric lineaments MEASURED, IN SHIPPED FILE

  • (a) GeoDAWN airborne radiometrics K, Th, U, total count (USGS 22103, DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ, public domain).
  • (b) Gradient magnitude, then an oriented matched filter (max over 12 orientations of a 15-px mean — a ridge detector, not a threshold).
  • (c) Potassium and thorium anomalies map clay/silica alteration and fracture-hosted fluid pathways along faults, including faults with no Quaternary surface expression.
  • (d) The family tried radiometric point statistics (feature-versus-background histograms) and generic gradient thresholds; an oriented ridge transform of the four channels is not in the corpus.
  • Measured. 0.0269 credit/cell at 20,000 cells on the SGMC-off instrument — 5.5× worse than the lidar scarp ridge, so it contributes volume only (weight 0.55 in the credit field).

H50-5 — cross-strike swath profiling of the 1 m DEM NOT STARTED, BLOCKED

  • (a) USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM tiles (716 tiles; 706 fetched by the sibling build).
  • (b) For each candidate corridor, extract a swath profile normal to the strike over 2–5 km, and score the *monoclinal step* shape (a persistent vertical offset across the swath) rather than local slope. This is the standard tectonic-geomorphology test for a fault scarp and is insensitive to roads, terrace risers and channels, which all produce symmetric steps locally.
  • (c) A fault with no mapped trace usually still has a metre-scale cumulative scarp; swath profiles detect cumulative offset, which point descriptors (as used by the family’s scarp layer) dilute.
  • (d) Nothing in the family profiles across strike; they use per-cell 2 m descriptors aggregated to 100 m.
  • Cost. High (needs the raw DEM mosaic, ≈100 GB tile set, and a full-survey swath sweep). Deferred: the 100 m aggregate of those descriptors is already in the shipped file.

H50-6 — focal mechanisms as a strike prior REJECTED

  • (a) USGS ComCat moment tensors / focal mechanisms.
  • (b) Weight each corridor by the number of mechanisms whose nodal-plane strike is within 20° of the corridor azimuth.
  • (c) Independent confirmation that a lineation is a fault plane and not an alignment artefact of location error.
  • (d) The family never used mechanisms.
  • Measured/rejected. ComCat publishes mechanisms for M ≳ 3.5, which in this survey is ≈40 events — three orders of magnitude fewer than the events the corridor detector consumes, and each is a catalogue fault event, biasing the prior toward mapped structures. Rejected on statistical power before implementation.
