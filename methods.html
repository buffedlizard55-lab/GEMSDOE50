Methods, sources & limitations

The H53 hypotheses were ranked and its leading test frozen before holdout scoring. Failed tests are reported without threshold tuning.

H53-A: shallow thermal point geometry + magnetic alignment

Within each GDR survey Area, select positive F2mDAB values at or above the within-Area 75th percentile. Join probes within 1.5 km, require ≥5 points, a PCA eigenvalue ratio ≥3, an 0.8–20 km axis, then require ≥50% of samples to be within 500 m and 30° of a high-strength/high-coherence GeoDAWN TMI_up150 ridge. Emit a 1.5-pixel Gaussian corridor; exclude predictions within 300 m of the supplied catalogue. No labels or prior prediction pixels enter the feature builder.

Result: 145 clusters tested; 0 accepted (113 too small, 24 insufficient TMI alignment, 8 insufficient PCA ratio). No parameters were loosened after this result. See the frozen preregistration and implementation.

Seismicity caution: H51-B already tested point/event geometry and was not validated; declustering, injection/mining confounds, event-location uncertainty and matched smoothed-density controls remain unresolved. It is not ranked here as a novel validated strategy.

Historical H51 method (archive only)

Field. Two independent physical families, each reduced to an oriented lineament strength with a multi-scale structure tensor (gradient outer-product coherence × gradient magnitude, robust-normalised inside the footprint), then summed with equal weights. No single layer is allowed to carry the ranking on its own.

Domain. Every emitted dot is more than 300 m from any provided-catalogue pixel. The staff clarification recorded by the sibling repositories (2026-09-16 / 2026-09-21) is that catalogue pixels are masked out of the scored truth and a prediction near a known trace but far from NEW truth is fully penalised, so catalogue contact is a pure cost.

Emission. Greedy packing by expected marginal credit with a 3-pixel suppression radius, because two dots closer than the metric's own 300 m kernel are nearly redundant while each still costs the 0.2 term.

Mass. The metric's own marginal condition (a dot pays iff its kernel credit exceeds 0.2 × DTI) applied through an owner-reported score and local SGMC-off comparison; the resulting marginal transfer of 0.28 is a historical model input, not an independently verified live score.

Location uncertainty (measured)

Only 10,107 in-survey events publish ComCat's own horizontalError; the other 61,215 are given the calibrated model below, fitted on 141,888 events that carry both the error and the quality metrics (R² = 0.715).

log10(h_err_m) = a0 + a1*log10(nst) + a2*log10(gap) + a3*depth_km + a4*mag

Footprint holdout: median predicted 3064 m against median observed 4810 m; within a factor of two for 50.40% of held-out events. This is this project's own construction, not part of the cited papers, and it is the weakest link in the seismicity layer.

Corridor width rule: max(2 * local 1-sigma location uncertainty, 300 m), then a Gaussian of that width so the metric's 300 m kernel can be used at emission.

What was tested and rejected in this session (negative results are kept)

  • Seismicity event-geometry corridors - level with the matched random control on the off-catalogue instrument, no Monte Cristo gain. Not given mass.
  • Magnetic and gravity lineament families - SGMC-off credit per dot below the random control at matched mass. Not given mass.
  • Orientation-concordance gate across all families - lowered both the SGMC-off credit and Monte Cristo coverage, concentrating mass on the largest structures. Not applied.
  • Depth-dip surface projection - ComCat's median depth error in this footprint is about 1.5 km, so a dip-driven surface offset would be an order of magnitude larger than the 300 m scoring kernel. Deliberately not applied.
