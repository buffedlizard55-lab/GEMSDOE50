Methods, sources, and limits

Earthquake locations generate candidate geometry; density is only a matched control. Exact constants were frozen before fault-proxy scoring.

H55-S1 pipeline

  1. Verify and filter. Check the Zenodo MD5 and schema; require relocated events, depth 0–25 km, and finite template cells; apply 2 km GDR-hot and 3 km explicit anthropogenic-event screens.
  2. Space-time thin. Keep one largest-magnitude event per 250 m UTM cell per 90-day Unix-epoch window, with event-ID tie break.
  3. Test point geometry. Normalize nearest-neighbor triangle area by squared distance to the 20th neighbor; compare with 32 uniform-footprint catalogues and keep empirical p≤0.20.
  4. Fit local axes. Full 2-D covariance in 5 km neighborhoods; require ≥10 events, ≥3 years, linearity ≥0.60, length ≥1.5 km, and 24-bootstrap p90 strike error ≤30°.
  5. Snap independently. Dynamic programming moves only cross-axis toward a 70% 3DEP scarp / 30% radiometric-gradient ridge, with orientation and continuity terms.
  6. Broaden and emit. Test 300/600/1,000 m half-widths, exclude supplied faults by 300 m and all prior positive pixels, then pack binary cells at 3-pixel spacing.
  7. Falsify. Compare with 1/2 km density, novelty-domain random, four translations, Candidate B, four macrofolds, and 16-subtile bootstrap.

H58-S1 pipeline (this session)

  1. Pin and screen. Verify the SHA-256 of the committed USGS ComCat extract (222,939 rows; official service FDSN event web service); keep tectonic types, M ≥ 1.5, depth < 25 km, horizontalError ≤ 10 km, in-grid events (93,934 in bounds).
  2. Remove known injection/mining sites. Drop every non-tectonic event type (545 rows) and apply a 3 km buffer around 522 explicit anthropogenic event centres (22,825 events removed). The GDR-1391 hot-feature CSV is absent in this environment; the screen is recorded as incomplete.
  3. Decluster. 2-D triangle-area background test against a randomized catalogue (unverified adaptation of Ouillon & Sornette 2011; 68,645 of 71,109 events kept).
  4. Fit local axes. Full 2-D covariance of each epicentre’s 10 nearest neighbours; keep linear (elongation ≥ 3.0), well-sampled (≥6 events, σ1 ≥ 800 m) neighbourhoods; 1,374 lineations accepted.
  5. Render corridors. Corridor along each principal axis with half-width = the catalogue’s own horizontalError (median 0.88 km — several pixels at 100 m: a corridor prior, not a trace).
  6. Emit. Variable-density blue noise at the metric’s own 300 m support; mass selected by the frozen transfer-modelled rule; support caps at 98,598 cells.
  7. Place. Every dot is offered to independent ridge targets (70/30 LiDAR-scarp / radiometric); the identity placement is kept because no snap improved the proxy.
  8. Gate. Off-catalogue scoring only (>300 m from the supplied catalogue); every prior positive pixel excluded; falsified against smoothed density, random, translations, the H56 incumbent, and the frozen four-macrofold holdout.

The 2-D triangle-area test is an unverified project adaptation, not the published 3-D tetrahedron method, and horizontalError is a scalar, not a per-event covariance (not an ACLUD reproduction, Wang et al. 2013). Mixed-network ComCat contributor rights are a flagged intra-repo conflict; see the ComCat rights review and the H58 preregistration.

H61 seismic-lineation method and sources

  • Catalogue: USGS ANSS ComCat via the FDSN event service (https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/query), hash-pinned extract SHA-256 19e726c8d0640ad0bdf9c239ae4a855dc1fef18956cfcc7ec9f34b45eb314c82. Field list: USGS CSV format page; flag: its horizontalError link (data/comcat/data-eventterms.php#horizontalError) currently redirects to the GeoJSON feed page, so the definition quoted in the preregistration (“largest projection of the three principal errors on a horizontal plane”, in km) could not be re-read at a live official URL on 2026-10-07. USGS-authored data are public domain (USGS copyrights and credits); contributions from non-USGS networks are not necessarily USGS-authored, so challenge-use rights remain unresolved.
  • Declustering: Zaliapin & Ben-Zion (2013), JGR 118, 2847–2864, doi:10.1002/jgrb.50179 — nearest-neighbour proximity with df = 1.6, b = 1; two-component Gaussian mixture threshold log10η0 = -3.951.
  • Background screen: 2-D triangle areas vs a coordinate-permuted reference (5th percentile, 2,563 of 12,450 kept). Unverified 2-D adaptation of Ouillon & Sornette (2011), JGR 116, B02306, doi:10.1029/2010JB007752.
  • Lineations: 12-neighbour inverse-variance covariance minus the mean squared horizontal error (2-D analogue of Ouillon, Ducorbier & Sornette 2008, JGR 113, B01306, doi:10.1029/2007JB005032); 74 neighbourhoods accepted, 31 after merging. Not an ACLUD reproduction (Wang et al. 2013, arXiv:1304.6912): ComCat gives a scalar horizontal error, not a covariance.
  • Corridors: epicentral (half-width = location error, 0.2–1 km) and two up-dip copies offset by z/tan 60° (half-width propagates depth error and ±10° dip, 0.2–2 km); dots snapped every 300 m to the H57 ridge across the corridor.
  • Prior corpus: scripts/fetch_prior_corpus.py — 50 hash-verified pins plus every TIFF in the 54 sibling repositories (receipt h61_prior_corpus_receipt.json).

Scientific limitations

  • 250 m/90-day thinning is not validated formal aftershock declustering.
  • No event-specific relocated covariance; widths are assumptions.
  • Mine/injection inventory is incomplete and not date-matched.
  • Epicentre axes can reflect dipping structures, swarms, induced activity, or network artifacts.
  • SGMC is incomplete proxy truth, not hidden labels.
  • A lineation is not proof of a surface fault or geothermal resource.

Protocol integrity

Five hypotheses and falsification gates were committed before H55 scoring. Pass 2 found a split-realization bug, fixed it, and reran everything. Prior-pixel exclusion was added as a restrictive, label-blind novelty hardening and logged as a deviation.
