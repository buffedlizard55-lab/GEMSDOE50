H57 executive summary

With binary unit dots the official metric is DTI = T / (0.2N + 0.8G + 0.2(T − M)) with M = Σ K(dot) (erratum, H59: this band originally said it “reduces exactly to T / (0.2N + 0.8G)”; that is exact only when T = M, and dots closer than 6 px do compete — H59 verdict §4), so a dot costs 0.2 whatever it earns and placement quality is everything. H57 is a greedy dot emitter at the metric's own 300 m support (3 px minimum Chebyshev spacing) over a belief field built from the two channel families that survive an elevation-stratified control: LiDAR scarp descriptors (step_max 2.73, lapneg_max 2.62, ex_mean 2.61, downface_max 2.53, ex_max 2.51, relief 2.41 pooled lift, 5/5 strata) and topographic steps (slope_max 2.61, hs_lineament 2.49, curv_prof_absmax 2.35, relief_local 2.27, slope_std 2.15, steep_frac 2.11, 5/5). The stratifier exists because the earlier screen's top channels were elevation itself (dem_mean 3.665, det_elev 3.657): F1's truth is USGS SGMC faults, which live in the mountains, so an unstratified screen was selecting terrain. The whole geodetic-strain family (0.28–0.38), every magnetic and radiometric channel (0.42–0.98) and every residualised 9×9 row are rejected by the same control.
