Executive summary

Leading method (shipped): build one structure-tensor saliency field per evidence family (detrended elevation and slope, 10 m topographic descriptors, 3DEP-1 m lidar scarp descriptors, airborne radiometrics from two independent mosaics, potential-field and geodetic-strain bands, and the competition’s own seismicity bands), rank each within itself, and emit dots where at least four families agree after a 200 m exclusion around the provided catalogue.

Every candidate is scored on an independent off-catalogue fault inventory with the competition's own distance-weighted metric, against matched-mass random controls in four spatially blocked folds, and compared dot-by-dot with every prior artifact this project can re-fetch; the emission budget is chosen from a published curve whose extrapolation is labelled as such.

What this is—and is not

This is a research proxy against existing mapped faults, not the hidden expert-labeled test set. A local pass is necessary for review but is not an organizer score or a promise of prize performance.

The Nevada catalog is CC BY 4.0, but it lacks event-specific location covariance. A precise-looking coordinate is not proof of a precise location; a hypocenter projection is not automatically a surface trace.
