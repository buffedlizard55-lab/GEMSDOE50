Why H33-B2 reportedly scored 0.2778

The exact artifact has 37,654 binary cells. GEMSDOE32 says it began with a 40,199-cell, owner-reported 0.2708 base and deleted all 2,545 cells at catalogue distance ≤2 pixels. For official triangular kernel k(d)=max(1-d/300m,0), weighted Tversky is T/[0.2T+0.2F+0.8G]. A no-credit dot adds false-positive cost; deleting one lowers the denominator without lowering T. Catalogue-near predictions are especially suspect because the target is faults omitted from the supplied catalogue.

That explains the plausible mechanism—sparse support and low-value pruning—but not the score attribution. The sibling audit calls the file UNSCORED and projects 0.274673. No organizer ID/hash crosswalk was found.
