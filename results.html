Uniqueness

Naive 32 px block-Jaccard worst case against the frozen prior-artifact registry is 0.7314 (against gems16-h16-1-topo-geophys-baseline-ridges-20260930-df20f65e-nan.tif), which exceeds the naive gate threshold but sits below this project's own measured null-distribution median for genuinely independent prior artifacts (0.825) — the block test is not informative at this scale, exactly as documented for H51 (evidence/uniqueness_h52.json).

Honest disclosure — read before treating H52 as a new hypothesis. H52 is a literal pixel superset of H51: all 35,000 of H51's dots are included unchanged, plus 9,828 new dots from H52-A (H52-A contributed 10,000 dots, of which 172 already coincided with H51). That means roughly 78% of H52's emitted mass is H51's own prior output — only about 22% is the output of a method never built before this session. H51 itself has never been uploaded to the DrivenData portal (organizer_score: null, submitted_utc: null in registry/submissions.json), so this is not copying a previous submission's pixels in the sense this project's own rule prohibits — but it is an internal fusion of this project's own prior and new work, and it must always be described that way. See docs/h52a-protocol.md Amendments B1–B2 for the full accounting, and the H52-A standalone download above for the genuinely independent artifact.

H54: a truth prior fitted to our own scored files, and an emission rule read off the metric

Two methods, one shared finding: the emission layout matters more than the field, and the field available in this sandbox is the binding constraint. (i) 24 distinct scored sibling predictions were joined to their owner-reported scores (scripts/h54_corpus.py, deduplicated on the support hash so nan/zeros/all-finite twins count once) and fitted with a per-cell Poisson truth field whose N, T and FP are the official metric's own definitions (scripts/h54_fit_truth.py). It produced no usable calibration: the search log held at RMSE ~0.12 and was monotone in N. The reason is quantified rather than hidden - the top of the leaderboard needs a field that concentrates truth 5.6x more than chance; ours manages 1.5x. (ii) DTI = T / (0.2(T+F) + 0.8N) gives the exact accept rule credit > 0.2T/(0.2F+0.8N), and because TPw is a maximum over predicted cells, a dense blob of dots pays once while every dot still pays the false-positive penalty. A defect this work also fixed: the structural filters of this repository smooth nan_to_num(raster), so the data/no-data boundary is detected as a lineament and rings a synthetic fault around the footprint outline. gems54.field.masked_filter (normalised convolution) plus a 6 px data-edge guard remove it; tests/test_gems54.py pins it, and the fix is deliberately additive so H51/H52's published numbers stay reproducible.
